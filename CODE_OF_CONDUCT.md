# Code of Conduct

Texas Communications Unit welcomes constructive participation from public-safety communications professionals, software developers, manufacturers, researchers, and other contributors.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others professionally and respectfully.
- Focus technical disagreement on evidence, requirements, and operational impact.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Protect sensitive public-safety and manufacturer information.
- Respect software licenses, intellectual property, and authorization boundaries.
- Avoid posting operational details that could create unnecessary safety or security risk.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate these expectations or create legal, security, or operational risk.

Concerns about conduct should be raised privately with Texas Communications Unit through an official organization contact channel rather than through a public issue when privacy is appropriate.
